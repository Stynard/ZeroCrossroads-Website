MarketRefine

Evidence over indicators. Guidance over decisions.

MarketRefine is a desktop market-evaluation application designed to help users identify publicly traded companies that deserve further consideration.

It brings together technical market evidence and structured evaluation to support independent research. MarketRefine is not a trade-execution service or a source of personalized investment recommendations.
